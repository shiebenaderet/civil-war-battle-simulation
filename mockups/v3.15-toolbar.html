Verdict

Pros: Reading level switch is one click, every time. Active state instantly visible. Best for classes where students experiment with levels.

Cons: Visually busiest — three pills compete with the masthead title. On a Chromebook at 1366px, it works; on an old 1024px netbook, it gets crowded. Pills stack to a second row on phones, costing vertical space above the battle screen.
