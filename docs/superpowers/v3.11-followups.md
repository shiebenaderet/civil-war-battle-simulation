## Recommendation

**One real BLOCKER:** the `body` background at `css/styles.css:143` references the dead `--color-navy-900` token. Without a `--color-paper` background on `body`, the Field Report aesthetic likely sits on a white page in regions where the page extends beyond cards (long scroll, mobile, print preview). This is a one-line fix and should be addressed before merge.

The other DEAD token references (intel/situation section borders) are visually subtler — they degrade to `currentColor`, which means the rules still draw a 3px line, just in the wrong color. Not strictly blocking, but the intel/situation accent stripes were design intent; **POLISH, ideally before launch**.

Everything else (stray Blooket hexes on section accents, Tailwind-color review badges, hover-lift on starter-chip and teacher-tip toggles, dormant `.section-title-line`/`h2` rules, dead `--shadow-glow` reference) is **DEFERRED** to a future polish version. None of it breaks layout or produces console errors at the file level.

**Go/no-go:** Conditional GO once `body { background: var(--color-paper); }` is fixed. Recommend also addressing the two section-border DEAD tokens (`:2596`, `:2650`) since they're trivially close in scope to the same fix. Visual QA by the human will catch any further regressions.
