# v3.19 — After-battle screen redesign + tiered battle logging

**Date:** 2026-05-30. From the teacher's play-through of v3.18 (checked through Antietam). Two changes.

## Change 1 — After-battle screen: bolder callout + tabbed deeper content

**Problem:** The "Key idea: write this in your journal" callout isn't prominent enough, and the Technology Spotlight (always shown) plus the stacked Voice / Bigger Picture sections make the screen long.

**Design:**
- **Bolder Key Idea callout.** Make the "write this in your journal" callout the visual anchor of the after-battle screen: larger heading, stronger accent, an icon/label that reads as an instruction. It stays always-visible for all tiers (its v3.18 job).
- **Small video thumbnail** sits directly under the callout (the Battlefield Tours card, rendered smaller).
- **One tabbed "Learn More" panel** replaces the three stacked sections. Three tabs, reusing the existing `.visual-tab` / `switchVisualTab()` pattern already used for Artwork/Battle Map:
  - **A Voice From the Field** (quote + attribution + source + explainer)
  - **The Bigger Picture** (narrative + perspectives). The old "Did You Know" trivia box is REMOVED (user decision 2026-05-30: it was the keyFact trivia the bold callout now replaces).
  - **Technology Spotlight** (tech name + description) — no longer always shown inline; now one tab
- Default active tab: **The Bigger Picture** (it carries the significance, closest to the key idea; user took the default). All three are opt-in by tab, so the screen is compact.

**Step-machine impact (js/ui.js narrative step machine):** Today step 3 reveals `sectionHappened` (What Happened + outcome + callout + tech + video), and step 4 reveals Voice + Bigger Picture (collapsibles). New flow: step 3 reveals What Happened + outcome + **bolder callout + video thumbnail**; step 4 reveals the **tabbed Learn More panel** (Voice/Bigger Picture/Tech). The tech box moves OUT of sectionHappened into the tab panel.

**Files:** index.html (restructure sectionHappened + replace sectionVoice/sectionBigPicture with a tabbed panel; tech moves into it), css/styles.css (bolder callout, tab panel styles, smaller video), js/ui.js (step-machine reveal logic; populate the three tab bodies; remove the old setupCollapsibleSection calls for voice/bigpicture, replace with tab init).

## Change 2 — Tiered battle logging (lower tiers log fewer battles)

**Principle:** Every tier PLAYS all 13 battles. Lower tiers only LOG (write about) a subset, to cut writing load for struggling writers. The reduction is on the handout, not in the game.

**Which battles each tier logs:**
- **★ Most Support (extra):** 4 battles — the act anchors (the reflection battle of each act): **Shiloh** (Act I), **Chancellorsville** (Act II), **Chickamauga** (Act III), **Appomattox** (Act IV). One per act.
- **★★ More Support (beginner):** 8 battles — each act's anchor + one more:
  - Act I: Bull Run, Shiloh
  - Act II: Antietam, Chancellorsville
  - Act III: Gettysburg, Chickamauga
  - Act IV: Atlanta, Appomattox
- **★★★ Standard (intermediate):** all 13 (unchanged).
- **★★★★ Extra Challenge (advanced):** all 13 (unchanged).

**Handout impact:**
- Most Support handout: Part 1 log shows only the 4 anchor battle rows (still grouped by act, one row per act). Act checkpoints + reflections + Part 3 unchanged. Much less to write.
- More Support handout: Part 1 log shows the 8 rows (2 per act). Otherwise unchanged.
- Standard + Extra Challenge: unchanged (13 rows).
- The "which battles" choice is intentionally the act anchors so the per-battle log lines up with the act reflection (which already centers on that anchor).

**In-game impact:** Optional/none required. The game still plays all 13 for everyone. (Possible nicety, NOT in scope now: on lower tiers, the "write this in your journal" callout could only nudge-to-handout on the logged battles. Defer unless requested.)
