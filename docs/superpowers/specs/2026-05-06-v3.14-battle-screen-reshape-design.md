# v3.14 "The Battle Screen, Reshaped" — Design Spec

**Date:** 2026-05-06
**Author:** Shie Benaderet (with Claude as collaborator)
**Roadmap position:** v3.14, fourth of five versions toward v4.0 launch
**Status:** approved for implementation planning
**Predecessor:** v3.12.1 (shipped 2026-05-06) — Acts of the War (recall + WWYD shuffle)
**Parent spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-05-04-launch-readiness-design.md`

---

## 1. Problem Statement

After v3.11 and v3.12, students leave the simulation with a real sense of the war as a documentary-styled story across four acts. But during each individual battle, the on-screen experience still feels like reading homework. The "way too much reading" complaint surfaced at the v3.11 visual checkpoint and never went away.

Diagnosis from re-reading the code: the problem is concentrated in **step 2 of the historical battle screen** ("What Happened"). On click of Continue from "Your Call," four sub-sections reveal at once with a 150ms cascade animation: WWYD Feedback → What Really Happened → Voice From the Field → Bigger Picture. By t=600ms the student is staring at a tall scroll-required document. Briefing (steps 0) and Your Call (step 1) and Reflect (step 3) are each lean enough; **the wall of text is inside step 2 specifically**.

## 2. Goals (and what we will NOT do)

### Goals
- A student reading a battle screen sees one focused thing per click, not a tall stack of revealed content.
- The reading load on step 2 is broken into three meaningful narrative beats (your choice's outcome, the battlefield events, and the broader meaning), each with its own pace.
- Active note-taking is encouraged through per-sub-screen nudges, with a safety net for students who didn't take notes when they reach the recall.
- The recall option-display bug from v3.12.1 (always-A) is fixed for recall the same way WWYD already is.
- No regression of the existing tutorial, help bar, save/resume, PDF export, or step pills.

### Non-goals
- No restructuring of Briefing, Your Call, or Reflect steps. Those are already lean.
- No re-authoring of existing battle content. The 13 battles' text in `battles.js` stays exactly as is.
- No new game modes.
- No new pedagogical scaffolding inside the reflection textarea (deferred to a future polish version).
- No external dependencies, no build step.

## 3. Scope of v3.14

Three coordinated changes in one version:

1. **Step 2 split.** "What Happened" becomes 3 sub-steps: Feedback → Outcome → Reflection from history. Each gets its own Continue button. The progressive-reveal stagger animation is removed.

2. **Note-taking and review system.** Per-sub-screen note-taking nudges; a per-recall-question "Review the act" escape hatch; a per-act synthesis-organized study guide accessible from both recall and reflection writing.

3. **Recall option shuffle.** The same deterministic-per-session shuffle pattern WWYD got in v3.12.1, applied to the 36 recall questions. Same `gameState.wwydShuffleSalt` reused.

## 4. Architecture

### 4.1 Narrative state machine extension

Today's `narrativeStep` ranges 0–3 (with 4 used as the "save and advance" step on reflection battles). After v3.14, it ranges 0–5:

| Step | Today | After v3.14 |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Briefing (Intel + Situation visible) | Briefing (unchanged) |
| 1 | Your Call (WWYD) | Your Call (unchanged) |
| 2 | What Happened (4 sub-sections cascade-revealed) | **Feedback** (your choice vs. history) |
| 3 | Reflect (only on reflection battles) | **Outcome** (What Really Happened + casualties + Tech) |
| 4 | Save and advance (reflection battles only) | **Reflection from history** (Voice + Bigger Picture) |
| 5 | — | Reflect (formerly step 3, only on reflection battles) |
| 6 | — | Save and advance (formerly step 4) |

The reflection-fire path's intercept for the recall moment (added in v3.12.1) shifts from case 3 to case 5 in the switch statement.

### 4.2 Step pills (visible to user)

The four-pill step indicator ("Briefing / Your Call / What Happened / Reflect") remains visually four pills. The "What Happened" pill is active across narrativeStep 2–4. A new helper `narrativeStepToPillIndex(step)` maps:

```
0 -> 0 (Briefing)
1 -> 1 (Your Call)
2, 3, 4 -> 2 (What Happened)
5 -> 3 (Reflect)
```

Optionally, the active "What Happened" pill displays a small dots progress indicator (○●○ → ●●○ → ●●●) showing internal sub-step progress. v3.14.0 ships without dots; they can be added in v3.14.x polish.

### 4.3 Step 2 split: what each sub-step shows

**Step 2 — Feedback**
- Visible: WWYD Feedback panel (badge, your choice, historical decision if different, detail text)
- Hidden: WWYD options section, all step-3+ sections
- Continue text: "Continue"
- Nudge: "✎ Worth writing down: [hint about what your choice meant]"

**Step 3 — Outcome**
- Visible: WWYD Feedback (still on screen, fading or just remaining), sectionHappened (What Really Happened + outcome box + Tech Spotlight)
- Hidden: WWYD options, sectionVoice, sectionBigPicture, sectionReflect
- Continue text: "Continue"
- Nudge: "✎ Worth writing down: [hint about a key outcome fact]"

**Step 4 — Reflection from history**
- Visible: sectionVoice + sectionBigPicture (collapsibles default-expanded since they're now this screen's main content), Did You Know box, perspectives
- Hidden: WWYD Feedback, sectionHappened, sectionReflect
- Continue text: depends on battle
  - Non-reflection battle, last battle: "Complete Historical Mode"
  - Non-reflection battle, not last: "Next Battle →"
  - Reflection battle: "Continue" (proceeds to step 5)
- Nudge: "✎ Worth writing down: [hint about voice/meaning]"

### 4.4 Cleanup of progressive reveal

The existing `reveal-stagger`/`reveal-stagger-1..4` CSS classes are no longer applied by JS. The CSS rules can stay for backward compatibility but become unused. The JS in current `case 2` that applies stagger classes to `feedbackEl`, `happened`, `voice`, `bigPicture` is removed.

Each sub-step uses a single fade-in transition on the newly-shown content. Reused class: `.fade-in-soft` (250ms opacity transition). New CSS, ~10 lines.

## 5. Components

### 5.1 Note-taking nudge

**Visual design:** small typewriter-styled line at the bottom of each What Happened sub-screen, between the content and the Continue button. Format:

```
✎ Worth writing down: the casualty number, over 22,000 in 12 hours.
```

CSS uses Field Report tokens: `font-mono` (Special Elite), `--color-ink-muted` color, with the pencil glyph (✎ U+270E) as a non-decorative leading marker. Always shown across all reading levels (per user direction).

**Storage:** inline in `js/data/battles.js` under each battle, in a new `notes` field with three properties (`feedback`, `outcome`, `reflectionFromHistory`), each with three reading-level variants. Example shape:

```javascript
notes: {
    feedback: {
        beginner: "Write down whether your choice matched what really happened.",
        intermediate: "...",
        advanced: "..."
    },
    outcome: { beginner: "...", intermediate: "...", advanced: "..." },
    reflectionFromHistory: { beginner: "...", intermediate: "...", advanced: "..." }
}
```

**Authoring volume:** 13 battles × 3 sub-screens × 3 reading levels = 117 nudge strings. Authored in v3.14.0 alongside code work, reviewed by user in batches of one act at a time before commit.

**Source citation:** each nudge object also has a `source` field pointing to the section of `battles.js` it draws from, parallel to recall question source citations.

### 5.2 Per-question recall review button + overlay

**Visual:** small "Review the act" link at the bottom of each recall question card, below the explanation footnote area, styled as `font-mono`, `font-size-xs`, underlined, in `--color-ink-muted`. Clicking opens a modal overlay.

**Modal overlay structure:**
- Backdrop: semi-transparent `--color-ink` at ~60% opacity
- Card: cream paper with sharp corners, max-width 720px, scrollable internally if content exceeds viewport height
- Header: act number + name, close button (×) top-right
- Body: synthesis study guide for the act (see 5.3)
- Battle thumbnail strip: top of body, 3-4 small images (one per battle in act) with name captions, sourced from existing `images/` directory
- Footer: "Close" button returning to recall (or click backdrop to close)

**State preservation:** opening the review overlay does not modify recall question state. The recall's `wrongAttempts`, `questionResolved`, current `questionIdx`, and selected button states all persist. Closing returns the student exactly to where they were.

**Z-index:** above main content, above help bar, below tutorial overlay (which is its own modal flow).

### 5.3 Synthesis-organized study guide (per act)

**Storage:** new `review` field on each act in `js/data/acts.js`, sibling to `intro` and `recall`. Three reading levels.

**Format (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced all use same structure, calibrated to level):**

```
## Act I Review — The War Begins

### The escalation
- Fort Sumter: Almost no one died. Both sides treated war as theater.
- Bull Run: Civilians brought picnic baskets. The Union army's panicked retreat shattered that fantasy.
- Shiloh: 23,000 casualties in two days. More dead and wounded than all previous American wars combined.

### What changed in expectation
- Before Sumter, many believed secession could be settled without serious bloodshed.
- After Bull Run, Lincoln signed bills to enlist 500,000 soldiers for three years.
- After Shiloh, Grant concluded only 'complete conquest' could end the war.

### What changed in how the war was fought
- Railroads moved soldiers faster than marching (Bull Run).
- Industrial-scale battle revealed itself at Shiloh.
- Civilians stopped being spectators and started being witnesses.
```

The headings are derived from the synthesis prompt at the top of each act's `groupedReflections` entry. For Act I the synthesis prompt asks "what caused this rapid escalation? How did the reality of battle change what people expected and how the war was fought?" — the three headings ("The escalation", "What changed in expectation", "What changed in how the war was fought") map directly to that prompt.

**Authoring volume:** 4 acts × 3 reading levels = 12 review documents. Authored in v3.14.0, reviewed by user before commit. Pattern: I draft Act I as a template, user approves, I draft Acts II–IV using the approved pattern, user reviews each.

**Same review accessible from reflection writing.** A "Review the act" link near the reflection textarea opens the same modal overlay. The student has the study guide where they actually need it most.

### 5.4 Recall option shuffle

**Implementation pattern:** identical to v3.12.1 WWYD shuffle.

```javascript
function getRecallDisplayOrder(actIndex, qIdx, n) {
    if (!gameState.wwydShuffleSalt) {
        gameState.wwydShuffleSalt = Math.floor(Math.random() * 1e9);
        if (typeof saveProgress === 'function') saveProgress();
    }
    var seed = (gameState.wwydShuffleSalt * 1664525 +
                actIndex * 22695477 +
                qIdx * 1013904223) >>> 0;
    function rand() { /* xorshift32 */ }
    var order = [];
    for (var i = 0; i < n; i++) order.push(i);
    for (var j = n - 1; j > 0; j--) {
        var k = Math.floor(rand() * (j + 1));
        var tmp = order[j]; order[j] = order[k]; order[k] = tmp;
    }
    return order;
}
```

**Render integration:** `renderQuestion()` walks the display-ordered indices, sets `data-original-idx` on each option button, click handler passes `originalIdx` to `onOptionClick`.

**Reveal text fix:** in the second-wrong path, `'The answer is ' + correctLetter` must use the *display* letter where the correct option appears, not `String.fromCharCode(65 + q.correctIndex)`. Compute `displayLetter = String.fromCharCode(65 + displayOrder.indexOf(q.correctIndex))`.

**Salt reuse:** uses the same `gameState.wwydShuffleSalt` as WWYD shuffle. One salt per session for both shuffles.

## 6. Failure modes and error handling

| Failure | Handling |
|---|---|
| Tutorial overlay tour references narrativeStep indices that no longer point at the right elements | Audit `tutorialSteps` array; update step indices to match new flow; one commit dedicated to this. |
| Save/resume of a save with `narrativeStep > 3` from an old build | Add clamp: `if (saved.narrativeStep > 5) saved.narrativeStep = 5; if (saved.narrativeStep > 3 && saved version is pre-v3.14) saved.narrativeStep = 2`. Or bump a save-version field and migrate. v3.14.0 ships with the simpler clamp. |
| PDF export captures responses keyed to currentBattle; advance-to-next-battle now happens at case 6 instead of case 3 | Single edit to `advanceHistorical()` call site in the new case 6. |
| Recall reveal letter mismatch (data index vs display position) | Acceptance criterion: in QA, second-wrong path on a question whose correct answer shuffled to position B must say "The answer is B," not "The answer is A." |
| Note nudge content collides with screen content | Author-time discipline: each nudge ≤ 140 chars, complementary to visible content, never duplicating a fact already on screen verbatim. |
| Review overlay traps focus when open (a11y) | Modal pattern with focus trap inside the overlay; ESC closes and returns focus to the "Review the act" link. |
| Review overlay z-index conflict with help bar / tutorial | Audit existing z-index values in CSS; review overlay sits at z-index 1500 (below tutorial at 1900, above modal at 1100). |
| Image thumbnails fail to load (offline classroom edge case) | Modal renders without thumbnails if any image 404s; bullet-point study guide remains. Same approach as battle screen artwork fallback. |

## 7. Acceptance criteria

For v3.14.0 to be considered done:

- All 13 battles have note-taking nudges populated for all 3 sub-screens at all 3 reading levels (117 nudges total).
- All 4 acts have `review` study guide documents at all 3 reading levels (12 documents).
- Step 2 split: clicking Continue from Your Call reveals only the Feedback panel; another click reveals Outcome; another reveals Reflection from history.
- Recall question display order shuffled deterministically per session; reveal letter matches display position.
- Review overlay opens from any recall question and from the reflection textarea screen; closing preserves recall state.
- Tutorial tour, help bar, save/resume, PDF export all function correctly through the new 6-step flow.
- No JS console errors during a complete walkthrough at any reading level for either side.

## 8. Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| 117 nudge strings is too much new content to draft to a high standard in the v3.14.0 timeframe | Medium | If nudge authoring slips, ship v3.14.0 with Act I nudges only and add Acts II–IV in v3.14.x patches. The split-screen refactor is independently shippable. |
| Step pill indicator confusing if "What Happened" stays active for 3 sub-steps without visual indication of internal progress | Low-Medium | v3.14.0 ships without dots indicator; add in v3.14.x if classroom feedback says students are confused about where they are. |
| Save/resume migration edge case: a save from v3.12.1 with `narrativeStep === 3` (today: reflect, after: outcome) loaded on v3.14 | Low | Clamp on load, and the user can always start over. |
| Tutorial breakage missed in QA | Medium | Tutorial walkthrough is part of the acceptance test; explicit check across at least one full battle from start of tutorial through final step. |
| Review overlay state preservation breaks if a student navigates inside the overlay (e.g., clicks a battle thumbnail expecting drill-down) | Low | Thumbnails in v3.14.0 are non-interactive (pure visual). Drill-down is a future polish. |

## 9. Out of scope (final, named explicitly)

- New game modes.
- New pedagogical scaffolding inside reflection textarea (sentence starters, etc.). Existing scaffolding stays.
- Teacher dashboard / live class view.
- Per-question analytics on recall performance.
- Drill-down interactions on review overlay thumbnails.
- Step pill dots progress indicator (deferred to v3.14.x).
- Adding nudges or review to free-play mode (free-play is post-launch unlock; out of v4.0 critical path).
- Localization beyond English.
